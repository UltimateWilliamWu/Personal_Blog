## 3) Scope statement（≤ 100 words）

**Scope (99 words):**  
The project shall deliver an AI-powered Research & Academic Equity Companion for UNSW students (with a focus on equity cohorts) to understand and synthesise academic literature. It shall be delivered within 12 months from 1 March 2026, under a budget of $350,000. It shall include semantic search integrated with UNSW Library systems; an interactive PDF de-jargoniser with plain-English explanations; citation-based knowledge graphs with debate filtering; a smart literature matrix with research-gap highlighting; and integrity and citation coaching (APA/Harvard/IEEE). It shall include WCAG 2.1 AAA accessibility and zero-retention privacy controls. It shall exclude multi-university adaptation and full-text hosting/redistribution.
